Previously On

When an episode starts after a long break from its series, the web client first shows what happened in the previous episodes. The text comes from the episode and season descriptions already in your library; nothing is looked up elsewhere. Only episodes before the one starting are shown, so nothing ahead is spoiled.

Days since the user last played anything of the series. 0 shows the recap every time (useful for testing).
